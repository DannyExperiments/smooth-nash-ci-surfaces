**Partial formalization.** For a two-by-two edge matrix over any field, the independent payoff equations identify all three row-base lines. Their intersections with the column-base lines consist precisely of the four coordinate axes and the checkerboard line in the affine cone. Generic smoothness, transversality, the remaining strata and the projective nonemptiness argument are in the manuscript. [Lean source](formal/closing/Certificate.lean)
