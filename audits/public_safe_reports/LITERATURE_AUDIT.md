# Q220 literature and original question

Search completed 2026-11-27. High confidence in the comparison with the named sources. Moderate confidence in the bounded negative search for an earlier proof of full complex-projective smoothness in this family.

## Exact target

The pinned catalog entry asks whether the Nash conditional-independence variety is a smooth complex projective surface for generic payoff tables when the binary graphical model has two disjoint edges and any number of isolated vertices. The thesis asks a broader question for arbitrary partitions and singles out surfaces. Its next three questions concern real components, the semialgebraic boundary and ED degree. Q220 specializes Question 3.6.1 and requires smoothness throughout the complex projective closure. [Catalog Q220](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/game_theory/noncooperative-games-equilibrium-theory.md#q220), [Sendra-Arranz, thesis §3.6, p. 196](https://tobias-lib.ub.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10900/160061/Thesis.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

The statistical graph specifies the factorization of the joint strategy. The generic parameter space is the full affine space of independent payoff tables, without graph-local restrictions. The complex projective variety is the closure of the rank equations off the tensor-coordinate hyperplanes and the total-mass hyperplane. The semialgebraic positive real locus is the set of totally mixed CI equilibria. Its smoothness is already known when it is nonempty. [Portakal and Sendra-Arranz, §2 and Theorem 12](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.13246), [Bouyer, Portakal and Sendra-Arranz, §§2–3 and Theorem 3.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.11467#S3).

## Boundary and rational surface checks

The older base locus calculations specialize to three lines per player in each edge's projective 3-space. Their fixed intersections survive for all payoff parameters. Bertini away from the base loci, or Bertini for arbitrary sections of the same line bundles, leaves the first-order rank in the actual payoff linear systems to be established. The candidate addresses it by separating generic tangent rank within a stratum from normal rank supplied by its base-player equations.

For a smooth surface in the candidate family with (r) isolates, summing the equation classes and applying adjunction gives canonical class ((r+2)(H_A+H_B)+(r+1)sum_i Z_i). This is ample, in agreement with the general-type conclusion of Corollary 28. The zero-isolate specialization of Lemma 27 is (mathcal O(2,2)). The (mathcal O(1,1)) expression in the proof of Corollary 28 does not follow from Lemma 27 and is unnecessary for the ampleness conclusion. General type rules out identification of the generic smooth surface with a rational or rational elliptic surface.

## Search limits and claim wording

Checked the thesis, the two peer-reviewed CI papers, the dependency-equilibria predecessor, the arXiv version history and author publication list, and exact-phrase and author/title searches for later or citing results on Nash CI surfaces, two disjoint edges, base loci, singularities and elliptic surfaces. The most direct follow-up found was arXiv:2511.11467v1. No earlier proof of the full boundary assertion appeared in the sources checked. This search leaves unindexed manuscripts, unpublished work and different terminology open.

The release claim is generic smoothness on the entire complex projective surface for the binary two-edge family. Earlier work receives credit for the equations, generic nonemptiness and irreducibility, dimension, intersection class, conditional general type, and smoothness of the open locus. The proof does not address Question 3.6.1 for arbitrary partitions, the existence or topology of positive real equilibria, their semialgebraic boundary, or Q221.
