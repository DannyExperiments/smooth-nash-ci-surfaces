# Mathematical audit

The internal review recomputed the payoff evaluation coefficients and the three base lines per player. At an open line, the base normal image has dimension two. At each of the four same-player intersection points it has dimension one. At the five mixed points, the row and column images are distinct two-planes of joint rank three. All these images are attainable at every point of the other factors because a nonzero coordinate monomial exists there. The two edge factors have disjoint cotangent summands.

For a product stratum of codimension \(c\) with \(b\) base equations, the nonbase incidence bundle has relative dimension \(2-c+b\) over the payoff parameter dimension. The review checked the application of generic smoothness over the target, the independence of tangent restrictions, and the codimension-one or codimension-two losses in each base payoff block. Each loss exceeds the corresponding possible generic fiber dimension. The argument therefore covers all points of a generic fiber.

The review checked the top Chern monomial separately for zero, one and at least two isolates. It also checked that restricting to the saturation boundary cannot contain any generic two-dimensional component. An independent finite-field diagnostic checked the local strata and differential ranks over the fields of orders 3, 5 and 7. These diagnostics illustrate the exact local calculations in the characteristic-zero proof.

Internally reviewed, not peer reviewed.
